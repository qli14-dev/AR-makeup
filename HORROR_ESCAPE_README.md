# 🏚️ THE TENANT - Horror Escape Room Game

A psychological horror escape room experience. You wake up trapped in Room 7B, an abandoned apartment where five people have disappeared over 30 years. Something in the walls is breathing. You have 30 minutes to solve the puzzles and escape.

## 🎮 How to Play

### Three Versions Available:

**⛪ GOTHIC CATHEDRAL VERSION (NEW - RECOMMENDED):**
```bash
open horror-escape-gothic.html
```
- **Underground Gothic cathedral atmosphere**
- Stone architecture with arches and columns
- Mixed teal/blue ambient + warm orange candlelight
- Blue-green fog drifting through space
- Wet reflective stone floors
- Floating light particles
- Sacred but corrupted medieval horror
- Volumetric lighting effects

**🎨 APARTMENT VERSION:**
```bash
open horror-escape-room-visual.html
```
- Modern abandoned apartment
- Realistic furniture with CSS graphics
- Flickering light bulb
- First-person room view

**📝 TEXT VERSION:**
```bash
open horror-escape-room.html
```
- Clean text-based interface
- Lighter on system resources
- Same puzzles and gameplay

### Gameplay:
1. **Click objects** in the room to examine them
2. **Solve puzzles** using clues hidden throughout the room
3. **Manage your inventory** - items appear in the bottom-left panel
4. **Escape before time runs out** - you have 30 minutes

## 🎯 Controls

- **Mouse Click**: Examine objects, interact with puzzles
- **ESC Key**: Close menus and modals
- **U Key**: Look up at the ceiling (secret)

## 🧩 Key Features

### Environment & Atmosphere
- Dim, flickering lighting with deep shadows
- Ambient sound design (breathing, creaks, whispers)
- Realistic abandoned apartment setting
- Psychological horror (no gore)

### Puzzle System
- 6 connected puzzles forming a logical chain
- Hidden clues throughout the room
- UV flashlight reveals secret messages
- Multiple puzzle types: codes, symbols, patterns

### Jumpscare System
- 6 different psychological scares
- Triggered by wrong actions or suspicious behavior
- NO GORE - focuses on atmosphere and implication
- 5+ jumpscares leads to bad ending

### Three Endings
- **Good Ending**: Escape successfully by solving all puzzles
- **Bad Ending**: Become trapped forever (5+ scares or time runs out)
- **Secret Ending**: Discover the true horror of the building

## 💡 Tips (No Spoilers)

1. **Examine everything carefully** - clues are hidden in plain sight
2. **Read all text** - journal pages contain crucial information
3. **Don't rush** - panic leads to mistakes and jumpscares
4. **Use the UV flashlight** - invisible messages are everywhere
5. **Think logically** - every puzzle has fair clues
6. **Pay attention to dates and numbers** - they matter
7. **Explore thoroughly** - move furniture, check drawers
8. **Listen to sounds** - audio cues hint at solutions
9. **Don't guess randomly** - wrong attempts have consequences
10. **For secret ending** - find ALL hidden messages

## 📖 Story (No Spoilers)

You're a journalist investigating disappearances at an old apartment building. You broke into Room 7B to investigate and were knocked unconscious. Now you're trapped, and something is watching from within the walls.

The previous tenant, Marcus Holloway, left behind puzzles. Solve them to prove your sanity and escape.
